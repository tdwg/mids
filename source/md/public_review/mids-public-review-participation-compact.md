# MIDS Public Review: How to Participate

The draft **Minimum Information about a Digital Specimen (MIDS)** standard is open for public review from **[START DATE] to [END DATE]**. Feedback is welcome from anyone working with natural science specimen data, in any discipline. All comments will be public and archived.

**Review manager:** Sharon Grant — [sgrant@fieldmuseum.org](mailto:sgrant@fieldmuseum.org)

**Normative (under review):** each information element's Element Name, Element URI, Label, Level, Definition, and isRequiredBy; the definitions of the MIDS levels, the disciplines, and the discipline schemas.

**Non-normative (informative):** each element's Mappings, Usage Note, Purpose, and Examples; the table of borrowed vocabularies.

1. **Read the draft** at [mids.tdwg.org](https://mids.tdwg.org/), especially the [Information Elements](https://mids.tdwg.org/information-elements/index.html) page (levels, elements, and Discipline Schemas).
2. **Choose a way to comment:** GitHub (step 3) or the feedback workbook (step 4). No GitHub account is needed for the workbook.
3. **GitHub:** sign in, search the [MIDS issues](https://github.com/tdwg/mids/issues), then comment on an existing issue or open a new one describing your proposed change and rationale.
4. **Feedback workbook:**
   1. Open the [MIDS Feedback Workbook](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/16nMgILgR6a4pkIRDR17n6WNvqCrnQbmprRSHbAxZGP8/edit?usp=sharing).
   2. Make a copy (*File → Make a copy*) or download it (*File → Download → Microsoft Excel (.xlsx)*).
   3. Fill in *Your details* on the **About** sheet.
   4. Add comments in the **orange** columns next to any entry; leave the **blue** columns unchanged. Use the **grey** rows to propose new entries.
   5. Email the file (or share your Google copy) with Sharon Grant at [sgrant@fieldmuseum.org](mailto:sgrant@fieldmuseum.org).
5. **Questions or anonymous comments:** email [sgrant@fieldmuseum.org](mailto:sgrant@fieldmuseum.org). To remain anonymous, say so explicitly in your email.
6. **Submit by [END DATE].** Later comments may be deferred to post-ratification maintenance.
7. **Share** this announcement with colleagues who should comment.
