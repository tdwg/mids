## Public Review 

Public Review has begun! The Public Review for the Minimum Information about a Digital Specimen (MIDS) Standard is currently underway and will remain open from [START DATE] until [END DATE]. Two ways are available to contribute during the review period: GitHub issues or the MIDS feedback workbook. Both count equally. Please read the following to figure out which is best for you.

MIDS defines four cumulative levels of digitization (MIDS0 Bare, MIDS1 Basic, MIDS2 Intermediate, MIDS3 Extended) and the information elements required at each level for Biology, Paleontology, and Geology collections. Feedback is welcome from anyone who digitizes, manages, publishes, or uses natural science specimen data, in any discipline. Comments from people outside the Task Group are especially valuable, and confirming that an entry is correct is useful feedback too.

### What is Under Review
The review covers four resources, all published on the [Information Elements](https://mids.tdwg.org/information-elements/index.html) page. Each resource has its own sheet in the [MIDS Feedback Workbook](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/16nMgILgR6a4pkIRDR17n6WNvqCrnQbmprRSHbAxZGP8/edit?usp=sharing).

| Resource | What it contains |
|---|---|
| **Disciplines** | The three collection disciplines MIDS covers (Biology, Paleontology, Geology), with definitions and sources. |
| **Levels** | The four cumulative MIDS levels (MIDS0 Bare, MIDS1 Basic, MIDS2 Intermediate, MIDS3 Extended), with definitions, notes, and purposes. Each level includes all information elements of the levels below it. |
| **Information Elements** | All 21 MIDS information elements, with definitions, purposes, usage notes, examples, and the disciplines that require them (isRequiredBy). |
| **Discipline Schema** | Which information element is required at which MIDS level for each discipline: one entry per discipline / level / information element combination. |
 
> *Important Note!*  
> Only these four resources are subject to review. Examples of information elements are non-normative. All other documentation, including the [mappings](https://mids.tdwg.org/mappings/index.html) and the [resources page](https://mids.tdwg.org/resources/index.html), is non-normative and, therefore, not under formal review. The purpose of the non-normative documentation is to improve understanding and clarify the normative documentation.

### How to Participate 
#### Explore the Standard
We offer several ways to explore the standard during the review.  
1.  Read the [MIDS Home Page](https://mids.tdwg.org/index.html) for an overview of MIDS, its aims, and how to use it  
2.  Read the [Information Elements](https://mids.tdwg.org/information-elements/index.html) page, which lists the MIDS levels, every information element, and the Discipline Schemas showing which element is required at which level for each discipline  
3.  Read the [Mappings](https://mids.tdwg.org/mappings/index.html) page to see how MIDS elements map to Darwin Core and ABCD terms using SSSOM  
4.  Try the tools on the [Resources](https://mids.tdwg.org/resources/index.html) page, such as the MIDS Calculator. Testing MIDS against your own collection data is an excellent basis for feedback  
5.  Read the [About](https://mids.tdwg.org/about/index.html) page for the motivation and history behind MIDS

#### Provide Feedback
1. **GitHub issues.** Sign in to GitHub (or [create a free account](https://github.com/signup)) and search the [MIDS issues](https://github.com/tdwg/mids/issues) to see whether your topic has already been raised. Issues labelled **MIDS Element** hold the working definitions of individual information elements. Comment on an existing issue, or open a new issue naming the element, level, or discipline concerned (e.g., "StratigraphicContext – Geology MIDS2") and describe the change you propose and why.  
2. **MIDS feedback workbook.** No GitHub account is needed, and you may submit anonymously. See [Participate Using the Google Sheet](#participate-using-the-google-sheet) below.  

#### Participate Using the Google Sheet
Participants may choose to participate using the MIDS Feedback Workbook, a Google Sheet, instead of GitHub. Feedback submitted this way can be anonymous.

1. **Make your own copy of the workbook.** Open the [MIDS Feedback Workbook](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/16nMgILgR6a4pkIRDR17n6WNvqCrnQbmprRSHbAxZGP8/edit?usp=drive_link). The shared file is view-only, so please do not request edit access. Instead, either:
   - create a copy in Google Sheets (*File → Make a copy*, requires a Google account), or
   - export the file as an Excel file (*File → Download → Microsoft Excel (.xlsx)*).
2. **Complete the feedback template.** Fill in *Your details* on the **About** sheet. Then, on the **Disciplines**, **Levels**, **Information Elements**, and **Discipline Schema** sheets, find the entries you want to comment on and enter your feedback in the **orange** columns on the same row (Feedback Type, Field(s) Concerned, Proposed Change, Rationale / Comment, and optionally a Related GitHub Issue). Leave the **blue** MIDS content columns unchanged. Use the **grey** rows at the bottom of a sheet to propose new entries.
3. **Submit your feedback.** When complete, either share your Google Sheet copy with Sharon Grant at [sgrant@fieldmuseum.org](mailto:sgrant@fieldmuseum.org) or send her the Excel file as an email attachment. Suggested subject line: *MIDS public review feedback – [Your name]*.
4. **To submit anonymously,** say so explicitly when you share the sheet or send the email. Your name and any identifying information will be removed before your comments are added to the public record.

For step-by-step instructions, see [How to Participate in the MIDS Public Review](mids-public-review-participation-detailed.md).

All comments will be public and archived following the review. Comments received after [END DATE] may be deferred and addressed through normal maintenance after ratification.

When the review closes, the Review Manager will compile every comment and record a recommended action for each. The MIDS Task Group will then address the comments and revise the draft before requesting ratification by the TDWG Executive Committee. To stay informed about meetings and updates, join the [MIDS Task Group mailing list](http://lists.tdwg.org/mailman/listinfo/tdwg-mids).

For questions, comments, or critiques, please contact Sharon Grant, Review Manager of MIDS, Field Museum of Natural History, at [sgrant@fieldmuseum.org](mailto:sgrant@fieldmuseum.org).

Standards live and die based on community. Without community support, a standard is just an organized list of random words. 
The public review is one of the best opportunities to support standards and the standardization process. 
You are free to leave public feedback or comment anonymously by emailing the Review Manager. If you wish to remain anonymous, say so explicitly in your email; your name and any identifying information will be removed before your comments are added to the public record.
