Public Review has begun! The Public Review for the Minimum Information about a Digital Specimen (MIDS) Standard is open for 60 days, from October 5th, 2026 until December 4th, 2026. Feedback is welcome from anyone who digitizes, manages, publishes, or uses natural science specimen data, in any discipline. Comments from people outside the Task Group are especially valuable, and confirming that an entry is correct is useful feedback too.

You can contribute through GitHub issues or the MIDS feedback workbook; both count equally. For step-by-step instructions, see the [Public Review](https://mids.tdwg.org/public-review/index.html) page.

### What is Under Review
The review covers four resources, all published on the [Information Elements](https://mids.tdwg.org/information-elements/index.html) page. Each has its own sheet in the [MIDS Feedback Workbook](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/16nMgILgR6a4pkIRDR17n6WNvqCrnQbmprRSHbAxZGP8/edit?usp=sharing).

| Resource | What it contains |
|---|---|
| **Disciplines** | The three collection disciplines MIDS covers (Biology, Paleontology, Geology), with definitions and sources. |
| **Levels** | The four cumulative MIDS levels (MIDS0 Bare, MIDS1 Basic, MIDS2 Intermediate, MIDS3 Extended), with definitions, notes, and purposes. |
| **Information Elements** | All 21 MIDS information elements, with definitions, purposes, usage notes, examples, and the disciplines that require them. |
| **Discipline Schema** | Which information element is required at which MIDS level for each discipline. |

> *Important Note!*  
> Only these four resources are subject to review. Examples of information elements, the [mappings](https://mids.tdwg.org/mappings/index.html), and the [resources page](https://mids.tdwg.org/resources/index.html) are non-normative: they clarify the normative documentation but are not under formal review.

### How to Participate 

1. **Explore the standard.** Read the [Information Elements](https://mids.tdwg.org/information-elements/index.html), [Mappings](https://mids.tdwg.org/mappings/index.html), and [About](https://mids.tdwg.org/about/index.html) pages, and try the tools on the [Resources](https://mids.tdwg.org/resources/index.html) page, such as the MIDS Calculator. Testing MIDS against your own collection data is an excellent basis for feedback.
2. **Comment on GitHub.** Search the [MIDS issues](https://github.com/tdwg/mids/issues) to see whether your topic has already been raised (issues labelled **MIDS Element** hold the working definitions of individual elements). Comment on an existing issue, or open a new one naming the element, level, or discipline concerned (e.g., "StratigraphicContext – Geology MIDS2") and describe the change you propose and why.
3. **Or use the feedback workbook.** No GitHub account is needed. Make your own copy of the [MIDS Feedback Workbook](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/16nMgILgR6a4pkIRDR17n6WNvqCrnQbmprRSHbAxZGP8/edit?usp=sharing) (*File → Make a copy*, or download as .xlsx), enter your feedback in the **orange** columns, and share or email it to Sharon Grant at [sgrant@fieldmuseum.org](mailto:sgrant@fieldmuseum.org).
4. **Comment anonymously** by saying so explicitly when you send feedback to the Review Manager. Your name and any identifying information will be removed before your comments are added to the public record.

### After the Review

All comments will be public and archived following the review. Comments received after December 4th, 2026, may be deferred and addressed through normal maintenance after ratification. When the review closes, the Review Manager will compile every comment and record a recommended action for each; the MIDS Task Group will then revise the draft before requesting ratification by the TDWG Executive Committee. To stay informed, join the [MIDS Task Group mailing list](http://lists.tdwg.org/mailman/listinfo/tdwg-mids).

For questions, comments, or critiques, please contact Sharon Grant, Review Manager of MIDS, Field Museum of Natural History, at [sgrant@fieldmuseum.org](mailto:sgrant@fieldmuseum.org).
